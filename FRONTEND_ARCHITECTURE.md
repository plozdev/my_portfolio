# UI Design Guidelines & Overall Structure

This document describes the overall layout, visual language, and section flow for the portfolio website. Use this as your guide for how the website should look and feel.

## 1. Project Overview
- **Owner:** Anh Hoang Mai (Software Engineer)
- **Goal:** Showcase professional experience, projects, and an engineering mindset through a modern, interactive, and visually stunning web interface.

## 2. Visual Identity & Aesthetic (The "Wow" Factor)
- **Theme:** Premium, modern, developer-focused, and slightly futuristic.
- **Color Palette:** A sophisticated dark mode. Use deep slate or charcoal for backgrounds. Use vibrant but elegant colors (like cyan, emerald, or violet) for accents, buttons, and highlights. Avoid flat, generic colors.
- **Depth & Texture:** Use "glassmorphism" (translucent cards with background blur) to create depth. Add subtle gradients and soft glow effects behind important elements.
- **Typography:** Clean, highly readable, modern sans-serif fonts. Ensure a strong hierarchy between headings, subheadings, and body text.
- **Iconography:** Use clean, consistent, minimalist icons for links, technologies, and contact info.

## 3. Page Flow (Top to Bottom)
The website is a single scrolling page. Design the screens to flow seamlessly in this order:

1. **Navigation Bar:** Always visible at the top. Needs links to major sections.
2. **Hero Section:** The introduction. Should be visually striking, with space for a 3D element and a clear call-to-action to scroll down.
3. **About Section:** A brief introduction to the developer's mindset and philosophy.
4. **Journey Section:** A visual timeline showing the path of their career and learning.
5. **Experience Section:** Detailed history of previous jobs and roles.
6. **Projects Section:** Showcase of built applications and software.
7. **Tech Section:** A visual grid or list of known skills and tools.
8. **Thinking Section:** A space for articles or engineering philosophies.
9. **Achievements Section:** Awards and certifications.
10. **Community Section:** Open source contributions and speaking events.
11. **Current Focus Section:** A highlighted area showing what the developer is doing right now.
12. **Contact Section:** Links to email and social profiles.
13. **Footer:** Copyright and minor links.
